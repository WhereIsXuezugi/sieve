How priority is used

A channel at +5 gets {{ (5 * settings.channels.manual_strength) | round(2) }} added to its rank score, which is enough to lift it past most of the page. A channel at -5 loses the same amount without vanishing, so you keep seeing the occasional one rather than pretending it does not exist. Blocking is the setting for "never again".

Affinity is derived, not set: it is your watch seconds on that channel, weighted by how much of each video you finished and decayed on a {{ settings.channels.affinity_half_life_days | int }}-day half-life, then scaled against your most-watched channel. It is worth up to {{ settings.channels.affinity_strength }} of rank on its own.
