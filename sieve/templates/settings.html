DeArrow

Crowd-voted titles and thumbnails from the DeArrow project, so you see what a video is actually about instead of what its thumbnail promises. Lookups go out by hashed prefix, so the server never learns which video you asked for.

A video whose title the community felt the need to rewrite is, by itself, evidence of clickbait. Sieve feeds that into the clickbait score rather than guessing from capital letters alone.

SponsorBlock

Segment data from SponsorBlock, used two ways: the player skips what you ask it to, and the proportion of a video given over to sponsor reads becomes a ranking signal.
