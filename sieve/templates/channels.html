How priority is used

Priority runs from −5 (rarely shown) to +5 (almost always near the top).

Watch time counts how much you finish a channel's videos, fading by half every {{ settings.channels.affinity_half_life_days | int }} days.

Exempt (allowed channels only): ticking it lets this channel's videos past your length limits, score limits and rules — use it for a channel you trust whatever your filters say. Unticking applies them again. It never overrides a block, a hidden video or a blocked word.
